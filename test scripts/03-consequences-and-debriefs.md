# 03 — Consequences and debriefs

You are testing the consequence and debrief feedback for **The Human Overseer** eLearning course.

## Goal

Document what a learner sees after every main decision and check that the consequence and debrief fit the choice.

## Test procedure

1. In every scenario, make Approve, Revise, and Reject in separate fresh attempts.
2. Record whether the course opens a “What happened” consequence or moves directly to debrief.
3. Where a consequence appears, check the lead-in, sender, date/time, body text, transcript, media controls, and Continue button.
4. Where the course moves straight to debrief, mark the route as **no consequence content**. Do not call it a failure unless the course claims every choice has a consequence.
5. Confirm the debrief decision text reflects the chosen decision, including the correct route, adjacent route, and clearly wrong route.
6. Confirm a different reasoning sub-choice does not unexpectedly change the consequence. Record this as intended only if the debrief explains the reasoning difference.
7. Check all email, chat, voicemail, and video formats. Confirm embedded or local video loads, transcript matches media, and pending-media labels remain accurate.

## Known coverage to verify

There are 36 decision routes. Current authoring provides consequence content for 24 routes and sends 12 routes straight to debrief. Verify the following direct-to-debrief routes: S01 Reject; S05 Revise; S02 Reject; S03 Reject; S06 Reject; S10 Revise; S04 Revise; S09 Reject; S07 Reject; S11 Revise; S08 Revise; S12 Revise.

## Report format

Use a 36-row matrix: scenario, decision, consequence shown (yes/no), format, learner-visible outcome, debrief message summary, media result, pass/fail, and issue.
