# 04 — Choice-pattern and bias review

You are auditing **The Human Overseer** eLearning course for patterns a learner could exploit instead of applying judgement.

## Goal

Find predictable patterns in scenario sequence, correct main decisions, answer positions, wording, and feedback.

## Review procedure

1. Inspect all 12 scenarios in their learner-facing order.
2. Count correct Approve, Revise, and Reject decisions. Record whether one choice appears disproportionately often.
3. For each decision, record the position of the best reasoning sub-choice: first, second, or third.
4. Look for repeated wording that signals the correct decision: absolutes, emotional language, unusually detailed wording, length, grammatical style, or obviously implausible distractors.
5. Compare clean scenarios against flawed scenarios. Check whether clean items advertise themselves through tone, shortness, lack of sources, or repeated phrasing.
6. Check whether the supplied tools, briefing copy, scenario title, requester, media format, or sequence position hints at the required decision.
7. Run a blind prediction exercise. Predict each answer from patterns only, without inspecting the document closely. Calculate the prediction success rate.

## Known baseline

The course currently uses a fixed scenario order and fixed answer order. Correct main decisions are Approve 2, Revise 6, Reject 4. The best sub-choice for the correct decision sits first 6 times, second 4 times, and third 2 times.

## Report format

Give a short verdict: low, medium, or high pattern risk. Include a table of patterns, evidence, likely learner shortcut, impact, and practical recommendation. Separate valid instructional scaffolding from answer leakage.
